Guns Score ETM does not collect or transmit personal data through gameplay.

What the game stores

The game stores your high score and music preference locally on your device. These values are not sent to the developer. Deleting the app removes its local data; device backups may retain copies under your Apple backup settings.

No accounts, ads, or tracking

The game does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or in-app purchases. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, or microphone. Gameplay works offline.

Support messages

If you choose to contact us through GitHub Issues, GitHub processes your account information and the content you submit. Your report is public and is used to respond to your request and investigate problems. Please do not include sensitive personal information.

This website and external links

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses, when you visit. This website adds no analytics or advertising scripts. Opening a support or privacy link from the game uses your browser and requires an internet connection.

See the GitHub Privacy Statement for information about GitHub’s processing.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from players, including children. Its content rating is provided separately in the App Store listing.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the game’s data practices change. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support tracker. Do not include personal information in a public report.
