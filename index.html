An offline zombie survival game

Thirteen guns.
One squad.
No exit.

Choose your weapon, command your squad, and hold out against the horde in Guns Score ETM.

Designed for landscape play on iPhone and iPad. Free, with no ads or in-app purchases.

Controls & support

Keep moving. Keep your squad close.

Move with your left thumb. Aim and fire with your right. Double-tap to dash, switch between thirteen weapons, and change squad orders when the battle turns.

No account or internet connection is needed to play. Your high score stays on your device.
