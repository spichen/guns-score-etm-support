Controls, troubleshooting, and a way to reach the developer.

How to play

  • Move: hold and drag on the left half of the screen.
  • Aim and fire: hold and drag on the right half.
  • Dash: double-tap on the left.
  • Change weapons: tap the weapon bar at the bottom.
  • Command your squad: tap the order chip above the weapon bar.
  • Pause or change music: use Pause at the top right.

Troubleshooting

If the game pauses after switching apps or a phone call, tap Play to resume. If you cannot hear music, check device volume and the music toggle on the pause screen.

If the game stops responding, close and reopen it. Avoid deleting the app unless you are happy to lose its locally stored high score and settings.

Contact & report a problem

Contact the developer through the support tracker below. Include your device model, iOS version, and what happened. A GitHub account is required to post.

Contact support
